# Round Robin Notes

Clinical documentation and patient-by-patient rounds for ICU teams.

## Language

**Clinical section**:
A named part of a patient's assessment and plan, such as NEURO or RESP. Its identity remains stable when its displayed title changes.

**L/D/A**:
Documentation for lines, drains and access. Older charts may retain combined skin and line findings in this section.

**SKIN**:
Skin and wound documentation recorded separately from L/D/A.

**Round**:
A team's ordered review of its patients, with a current patient and an explicit completion state.

**Round navigation**:
The transitions between Round Home, Patient Focus and End Round, subject to unresolved documentation and decision review.

**Print preferences**:
A clinician's choices for the layout and appearance of printed or exported rounding documents.

**Decision Scribe**:
Proposed documentation captured during rounds that requires clinician review and acknowledgement before completion.
