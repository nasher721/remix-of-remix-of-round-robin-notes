Colour

One green carries action, growth and reliability. The neutrals are hue-shifted toward it so chrome and accent read as one family.

Brand

Signal scarce highlight — always takes ink text

Neutrals green-cast, not pure grey

Semantic roles live values — these follow the active theme

The measurement that shaped the palette

White on #00C56A is 2.28:1 — a fail. Near-black on it is 8.43:1. So --lx-on-brand is ink in both themes, and the primary button is dark text on vibrant green. The lime already followed that rule; the green now does too.

Why figures are tabular

Proportional digits shift width as values tick, so a KPI reflows and a money column stops aligning. Every figure in this system sets font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums.

One bright, rest quiet

When one value is the answer and the rest are context

Categorical ramp

For genuinely peer-level series only

Light steps sit deeper than the equivalent UI colours so every mark clears 3:1 on a white card — green-500 is 2.28:1 there, and lime is 1.23:1. That is why the lime signal is a fill-with-ink-text and a dark-surface accent, never a mark on white.

Radius, space, elevation

Radius is assigned by role, not by size. Two cards with different radii is the fastest way to lose the look.

Radius by role

control · 12
tile · 16
card · 20
panel · 24
chip · pill

Space · 4px grid

Card padding is 20. Grid gutter is 20–24. Staying on those two is most of what makes screens feel like one product.

Dark mode drops these to near-nothing and builds depth from a surface ladder instead — shadows on near-black are invisible. Toggle the theme and watch the cards separate by lightness, not shade.

Provenance

Behance, Dribbble and rondesignlab.com are blocked at the network layer in the environment this was built in, so the source renders could not be viewed. The system is reconstructed from RonDesignLab's own published rationale for Ledgerix CRM — FMS — the Urbanist typeface, and a vibrant green for action, growth and reliability against neutral black, white and grey — then engineered out into a complete, internally consistent system. Faithful to the stated design language, not pixel-matched to the renders. The README's Tuning to the source section closes that gap from a screenshot in about five edits, all of them in tokens.css.
